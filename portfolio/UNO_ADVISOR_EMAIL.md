## Notes for you (not part of the email)

- **Lead with the AI degree.** UNO's BSAI is administered by the CS department and is Nebraska's
  first — your multi-agent-orchestration story is strongest there.
- **Both mechanisms are for *current* students.** Expect the answer to be "enroll, then work with
  us." That's normal; the repo accelerates you *inside* the program.
- **Be ready to defend it live.** If they offer a prior-learning evaluation, there will likely be
  an interview. Re-read your own `laws/` decisions and the audits beforehand so you can speak to
  *why* each major call was made.
- **Don't over-claim.** Ask what maps to what; let the department tell you which courses your
  evidence covers. Claim only what you can personally demonstrate.
