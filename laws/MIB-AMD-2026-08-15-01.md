## What the taxonomy already holds, and what it misses

Amendment VIII gave us the **False Green**: a check that reports protection it does not perform.
The 851 vacuous tests. The placeholder TWAP returning a constant. `MockCbETH.withdraw()` against
an asset with no on-chain redemption.

PHYSICS_of_law's standing test added **disclosed ungated area** — a place the law deliberately
does not reach, said out loud. And the migration near-miss added the **unmapped surface** — no
lie, no gate, and nothing shaped to notice one was missing.

**None of the three covers a check that passes correctly and then stops.**

The market cap tracked truthfully for nine minutes. Every tick inside that window was accurate.
No test lied. No gate was missing. And the number on the console was frozen for a month, because
the pass had a **bound nobody had named** — the time-stop measured age from birth rather than
from qualification, so tracking was already most of the way through its budget the moment it
began.

That is a distinct class, and it is the most expensive one in this protocol's record: **a green
whose timer had not run out at the moment of observation.**

## Core Principle 7 — Ratification by Bounds

**A pass is not ratified until its bound is stated.**

Every green has a condition under which it stops. If that condition cannot be named, the green
has not been measured — it has been *caught early*. Observing correct behaviour proves the
behaviour occurred; it does not prove the behaviour persists, and the two are routinely confused
because they produce identical evidence at the moment of observation.

The question is therefore not only "does it work" but **"what makes this stop, and have I seen
that limit?"**

### The operational form

A ratified claim carries its kill condition alongside its result.

| unratified | ratified |
|---|---|
| `posted=45` | `posted climbing · bound: token match holds` |
| "the socket is up" | "socket up · bound: linked wallet ≥ 0.02 SOL, degrades silently below" |
| "the keeper is live" | "keeper live · bound: gas balance, currently 0.038 SOL" |
| "tracking works" | "tracking works · bound: time-stop window, anchored to qualified_ms" |
| "the tape costs nothing" | "tape free · bound: ACTIVE_MINTS empty; any mint set resumes metering" |

### Why it is enforceable rather than advisory

Because in every instance in this protocol's record, **the bound existed and was discoverable
before the failure.** Not one was unknowable at ship time:

- the mc ceiling was a variable choice at `screen.ts:673`
- the PumpPortal stream's bound was published in the vendor's own docs
- the keeper's bound is its balance, readable on chain
- the tape's bound is a variable in its own banner

The bound was always in reach. It was never asked for. That is what makes this a discipline and
not an aspiration — the question has an answer every time, and asking it costs one line.

### What it does NOT require

It does not require proving the bound will never be reached. It requires **naming it**. A bound
that is known and accepted is a ratified pass; a bound that is unknown is not a pass at all.

Nor does it require a new test framework. It requires that a reported result state its own
expiry, the same way §I.2 requires that an untested feature be reduced or removed.

## Relation to the existing principles

**Position 1 (honesty as root)** — an unbounded green is not dishonest, but it is *incomplete in
a way that reads as complete*. Same surface, missing half.

**Position 2 (if it can't be tested it doesn't exist)** — this is that principle on the time
axis. A thing can exist for nine minutes and pass its test. Existence was never the whole
question; **duration** is the other half.

**Position 6 (no softening)** — the correction is expensive here. Stating a bound often reveals
that a thing thought finished is not, and that is precisely when the discipline is load-bearing.

---

## Cost of its absence, stated by the Architect

Three weeks. In this protocol that is the distance from concept to deployment to V2 — the same
span that produced PLATE V1 from an unaudited file to live mainnet.
