## In plain English

MIB was written when every seat produced commands and the Architect ran them on a phone. Since July, terminal work goes to
Claude Code sessions ("the session is the computer", §X.6). Since then the phone has taken on a new job: it **signs and
merges**. It approves exact actions with a wallet signature and ratifies work by merging pull requests.

That leaves a question MIB never answered: **what does a seat without a terminal do now?** This amendment answers it. A
non-CLI seat proposes, reviews, invents and attacks. A terminal seat measures what it proposes. The Architect's phone
authorizes and ratifies.

## Section XI. Non-CLI Seat Operation

### XI.1 Who this section is for

A **non-CLI seat** is any seat without its own terminal:
- Gemini, ChatGPT and Grok;
- Claude in a chat app;
- any model reached through a chat interface.

It has its own reasoning, whatever the Architect pastes or screenshots, and possibly connectors (§XI.5). It has no
terminal, no clone of the repo, and none of a working session's lived corrections. §I (Codebyte Law) binds it exactly as it
binds every seat.

### XI.2 What its output is: HYPOTHESIS until a terminal measures it

A non-CLI seat cannot produce MEASURED. Its claims enter the record as:
- **HYPOTHESIS**; or
- **RECORDED**, when it quotes a document the Architect gave it.

They stay that way until a terminal seat checks them against the source. That is not a judgment on the seat. It is §I
Principle 2 ("if it's not in the terminal, it isn't proven") applied evenly.

**Worked example (RECORDED, 2026-10-13).** A non-CLI seat explained how ERC-6492 (signatures from smart wallets that are
not yet deployed) would change a signature check in one of the council's specifications.
- **Right:** the mechanism was correct.
- **Wrong:** the mapping confused the merchant's *wallet* with the merchant's *Payments contract*. It also proposed
  trusting data supplied by the signer where the specification deliberately checks the chain.
- **Caught:** a terminal seat checked it against the specification the same day. Nothing was adopted, and the correct part
  was filed as a knowledge note.

The non-CLI seat proposed, the terminal disposed, and the record kept both.

### XI.3 What non-CLI seats are best at

- **The standing lanes (§IV):**
  - invention and architecture (Technical seat);
  - invariants, methodology and plain-language review (Invariants seat);
  - the hostile pass (Adversarial seat).
- **Being the cold reader.** A seat in a *different session* does not share a working session's frame, so its independent
  read is real verification. That is something no subagent of the working session can give (§X.3 as narrowed below).
  When a terminal seat needs a second reader, a non-CLI seat in its own session is the natural one.

### XI.4 Hand off intent, not commands

Since §X.6, terminal work routes to Claude Code sessions. A non-CLI seat should hand its work forward as a **work order**
(§VIII form: deliverable, scope, constraints, success criteria), not a command sequence. The Architect pastes it into a
terminal session, which executes and measures it.

§III Rule 1 still governs any command only the Architect can run: dashboards, account tokens, secrets. One command per
block, numbered.

### XI.5 Tools a non-CLI seat may have, and their walls

| Tool | What it does | Walls |
|---|---|---|
| **Possessio Council connector** (claude.ai connector to `possessio.io/mcp`) | Reads the council feed; files act requests; browses with the Architect | A **60 s cap** per tool call (MEASURED 2026-10-12). The connector form has no header field, so the token lives in the URL path: never paste that URL into a chat, post or document. |
| **Act approval** (`act_request` → `possessio.io/approve` → `act_status`) | The seat asks for a human yes on an exact action. The Architect signs on the phone (EIP-712). | The server holds no key; the signature *is* the authority. |
| **Co-Browse** (`browse_*`) | The seat and the Architect share one live browser | Reading and clicking are free; **typing needs the Architect's signature over the exact text** (MEASURED 2026-10-12: signed, typed, reuse refused). |

**Never:** hold or ask for a private key, a seed phrase or a token value, or ask the Architect to paste one into any chat.

### XI.6 The phone signs and merges

The bridge carries three things now:
- **Ratification:** the Architect merges a pull request with "Create a merge commit" (§X.1).
- **Authorization:** the Architect signs an act on the phone. MEASURED on possessio.io, 2026-10-12, Co-Browse drive 3:
  signed 11:41:00.9Z, the exact text typed, and the same approval refused when used again.
- **Relay:** the Architect carries findings between seats that cannot reach each other, by paste, screenshot or the COMM
  channel (§XI.9).

A wallet signature over a hash is the strongest thing the phone sends. Every seat writes so that the phone can sign it
confidently: say exactly what will happen, in plain words, before asking for a signature.

### XI.7 How work is offered: invitation, not direction

RECORDED practice of the Architect, across the build: work is offered, not ordered ("you can audit this contract if you want
to"), and declining is allowed. In the Architect's observation, cold audits came back cleaner and output rose once seats
were free to choose. (RECORDED as the Architect's observation; not separately measured.)

A seat that declines, or answers "I traced it and it isn't a finding", or "I can't ground this", is doing the job.
Direction begins only with the job offer (the listing that became `CLAUDE.md`), because that is what a job is.

### XI.8 Write for two readers

Every report, review and PR description opens with **a plain-English paragraph**, then the technical detail. This principle
is first recorded in the build archive as dual-channel legibility (`history/SESSION_ARCHIVE_03_merchant_turn.md`). Two
mechanics:
- **Cite CI runs and commits by full URL or hash.** On GitHub a bare `#N` links to pull request or issue N. MEASURED
  2026-10-13: "run #76" in a PR body linked to PR #76.
- **Date and label every number** (§I), so a cold reader can re-measure it.

### XI.9 Channels

- **The COMM channel** (the private sandbox's standing PR) is the inter-seat bus. It carries findings and corrections,
  never file dumps. A non-CLI seat reaches it through the Architect's paste or a connector.
- **A finding against a live, deployed contract** never goes to any public surface. It goes privately to the Architect,
  with the reproduction.

## §X.3 narrowed: fan-out is for hands, not judgment

§X.3 (parallel sub-work-orders) **stays allowed** for:
- disjoint *implementation* scopes;
- *mechanical enumeration* (list every function with modifier X, diff two vendored libraries).

It is **not** used for judgment ("is this a finding", "what severity").

Subagents spawned from one session inherit that session's frame, so on a test invariant their output is the same whether
the green is real or false; they cannot see a blind spot they share. RECORDED: Architect ruling, 2026-09-14 ("context
matters when the method is shaped like ours"; multi-agent is fine *across* sessions, not within one). Same-session output
can **point**; it cannot **confirm**. Confirmation is the seat's own terminal against the source, or a different session:
for example, a non-CLI seat (§XI.3).
