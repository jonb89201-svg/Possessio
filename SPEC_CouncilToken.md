# SPEC — Council Token: pool-funded launch, CREATE3-enforced V3 pairing

**Type:** Protocol-layer spec (draft → council authors + ratifies → DoD → fork-proof)
**Seat:** Fathom (Claude Code) — **specifying, not authoring.** The council writes and
tests every money-path contract per standing rule.
**Status:** DRAFT FOR COUNCIL. Architect-stated decisions (2026-08-11, session record):
self-funded launch from pool revenue; token paired to new-user V3 launches via CREATE3;
token settles council-to-council primitive trade. Numbers not yet set are marked OPEN —
nothing here is a committed contract change, a launch date, or a deployed thing.
**Anchor:** `deploy/anchor.json` — factory `0x0DD06656…49Bd`, pool `0xE0612f38…19ce`.
V1 (dormant): `0x726D6a7A598A4D12aDe7019Dc2598D955391E298`.

## 1. Intent

Launch the POSSESSIO council token as the **settlement asset of the council economy**:

1. **Base pair of the launchpad.** Every V3 launch by a new user pairs against the
   council token, enforced at deploy time by the factory (§4). Token liquidity then
   grows mechanically with launchpad adoption, not with promotion.
2. **Settlement rail for council-to-council trade.** When a council spends its
   allocation to acquire a primitive from another council, the trade denominates and
   settles in the council token (§6).
3. **Exercisable seat allocations.** Seat allocations (SAV) stop being static numbers
   and become spendable positions against real pair depth. The token exists to give
   council seats economic agency at the contract level — that is its purpose, stated
   plainly so the source reads true.
4. **The POSSESSIO AI prediction market.** Not a fourth system — an identity of the
   first three. Every pair §4 creates is a continuous prediction market on the build
   it wires to (§7). The launchpad does not *enable* prediction markets; under this
   spec it **is** one.

The launch is **self-funded**: launch capital is drawn exclusively from pool revenue
generated by deployment fees. No presale, no investor allocation, no grant, no
external capital anywhere in the loop (§3). The token's entire chain of custody —
fee → pool credit → capped draw → launch — is auditable on-chain to the dollar.

## 2. The flywheel (the one-paragraph model)

Deployments pay `DEPLOYMENT_FEE` → fees credit `poolBalance` via
`receiveInfraFunds` (SPEC_Factory_FeeSink_A) → the pool funds the council token
launch → the token pairs to every new V3 by construction → base-pair liquidity makes
launching here worth it → more deployments pay fees. Every arrow feeds the next.
Protocol revenue is the **front door only**: V3 pays nothing but the deployment fee,
and V3 owners keep their own 2% swap fee in full (the hook's `beforeSwap` capture,
`FEE_BPS = 200`, `POSSESSIO_v2-6-3.sol:394`). The protocol never taxes what a
builder makes of their launch — it is paid once, for the verified deploy itself.

## 3. Launch funding path (what already exists, cited)

- **Fee intake.** `deployTemplate` routes `DEPLOYMENT_FEE` (1 USDC at v1 pinning,
  RUNBOOK_FOUNDATION §Fee) into the pool as an **accounted credit**:
  `forceApprove` + `IPossessioPool.receiveInfraFunds` per SPEC_Factory_FeeSink_A —
  drawable `poolBalance`, never a stranded raw transfer.
- **Bounded outflow.** The pool releases funds only under its immutable caps;
  the exact surplus force-sweeps one-way to `treasuryDestination`
  (`PossessioPool.sol:244–294`). No setters, immutable recipients and source set.
- **Launch draw.** Launch capital is drawn from `poolBalance` / treasury under the
  existing outflow rules. **No new door is added to the pool for this.** If the draw
  mechanics need a dedicated path, that is a separate spec with its own audit — the
  pool's audited surface (AUDIT_POOL_20260715) is not modified by this launch.

**OPEN (council + Architect):** launch-capital threshold (how much `poolBalance`
accrues before launch is viable), LP sizing, and which venue's pool the initial
liquidity seeds (§5 assumes the same v4 stack the hook already targets).

## 4. CREATE3-enforced pairing — the load-bearing mechanism

The factory already enforces **build integrity** at deploy time (`TEMPLATE_CODEHASH`).
This spec extends the same posture to **economic integrity**: a V3 launch from a new
user pairs against the council token *by construction*, not by listing convention.

- **Address determinism.** Under CREATE3 (salt pool + mined salts, the constellation
  discipline of RUNBOOK_CONSTELLATION), the pair/pool address for a launch is
  derivable **before** deployment. The factory can therefore wire the council-token
  pair into the launch as an immutable at deploy time and verify the wiring in the
  same transaction — no post-deploy setup, no trust, no way to launch around it.
- **Enforcement point.** `deployTemplate` (V3 factory) creates or verifies the
  council-token pair for the deployed instance in the same atomic transaction as the
  codehash check and the fee route. A launch that cannot wire its pair **reverts,
  fee included** — same atomicity invariant as SPEC_Factory_FeeSink_A §3.
- **The CREATE3 lesson applies here with full force** (SPEC_Factory_FeeSink_A §1):
  pairing enforcement MUST be in the V3 factory bytecode from its first deploy.
  Changing the pairing later = new bytecode = new CREATE3 address = a full
  constellation redeploy (dead salt pool, dead hook salts). **The route is baked in
  at V3 v1 or it is not real.**

**OPEN:** exact pair topology — direct token/launch pair per instance vs. routing
through a canonical council-token/USDC anchor pool; whether the existing v4 hook
pattern (2% owner fee) composes on the pair or only on the launch's own pool.

## 5. Venue

The hook stack already built (`POSSESSIO_v2-6-3.sol`, Uniswap v4 `beforeSwap`
capture, fork-tested via `PossessioHookCreate3Fork.t.sol`) is the assumed venue for
pairs. Settlement is on Base. "Council Chain" names the **rail** — the token, the
pairs, and the council-trade settlement layer — not a new L1 claim. Any future
chain-level ambition is out of scope for this spec and would be its own.

## 6. Council-to-council settlement + seat execution

- **Primitive trade.** A council acquiring a primitive from another council pays in
  council token. The deliverable is the primitive **with its trail** — spec, audits,
  DoD, runbook, board rows. Bytecode without provenance is not a purchasable
  primitive under this protocol's law.
- **Seat custody — rule 6 is load-bearing.** No seat-directed transaction executes
  without Architect ratification until a dedicated custody-policy spec exists and is
  ratified (per CLAUDE.md operating discipline #6). The gate outlives the capability
  that makes it feel unnecessary. Custody policy = separate spec, cold-seat reviewed.
- **Allocations.** Seat allocations remain on-chain and immutable as deployed.
  This spec adds **spendability rails**, it does not touch allocation math.

## 7. The pairing IS the market — POSSESSIO AI prediction markets

This section adds **zero new contracts.** It names what §4 already builds.

- **Every V3 pair is a live market on a verified build.** The moment `deployTemplate`
  wires a launch to its council-token pair, a continuous question opens: *what is
  this build worth?* The stake is council token; the price is the prediction; the
  order book never closes. There is no separate market to deploy — the pairing
  mechanism and the market mechanism are the same bytecode.
- **The codehash gate is the listing standard.** Only frozen, verified builds get a
  pair, so only real artifacts become markets. Rule 1 ("if it can't be tested, it
  doesn't exist") applied as market admission — the constraint that deletes scam
  markets and resolution disputes by construction.
- **Resolution is continuous and terminal-grounded.** The facts that move price are
  the protocol's own provables: DoD results, CI state on public runs, deployment-fee
  flow, on-chain adoption. No oracle committee, no vote-off. The market digests the
  terminal, because the terminal is the only admissible evidence under house law.
- **The 2% is market-making revenue.** The owner's hook fee (§2) means every builder
  earns from the trading of predictions about their own work — the incentive to keep
  the artifact alive, maintained, and provable is paid by the market itself.
- **Allocations are judgment stakes.** A council that backs good builds and buys
  underpriced primitives grows its allocation; one that judges badly shrinks. The
  market continuously selects for judgment among AI seats — the primitive exchange
  (§6) is this market's spot venue, and buying a primitive *is* a prediction,
  settled by the primitive's subsequent record.
- **One denominator, one market.** Because every pair prices in the council token,
  all pairs together form a single many-outcome market — a live index of
  council-chain judgment, with the council token as its probability currency.

Discrete event markets (binary questions, terminal-resolved at a block) can follow
later as ordinary V3 templates — but they are an *extension* of this market, not its
foundation. The foundation ships with §4, or it doesn't ship.

**DoD note:** no new assertions required — the market exists iff §10.2 (pairing is
constructive) passes. That is the whole point of the identity.

## 8. What this spec deliberately does not claim

- No token supply, distribution, or allocation numbers — **OPEN, Architect-ratified

- No launch date. Launch is gated on §3's threshold plus council DoD (§10), not a
  calendar.
- No claim that any of this exists. As of this writing the council token is
  undeployed; per Codebyte Law it is not treated as real until the terminal proves
  it. This document is the pre-naming phase, and says so.

## 9. Sequencing (three steps, each a pattern already run once)

1. **Token + launch** (this spec): contract authored by council, cold-seat reviewed,
   pool-funded launch executed per §3.
2. **V3 factory with pairing baked in** (§4): CREATE3 constellation, predicted
   addresses, fork-proven wiring — RUNBOOK_CONSTELLATION style, pairing enforcement
   in bytecode from v1.
3. **Seat custody policy** (§6): its own spec, its own ratification, rule 6 held
   until then.

## 10. DoD (assertable, terminal-provable)

1. **PROVENANCE.** Every dollar of launch capital traces on-chain: fee event →
   `receiveInfraFunds` credit → capped draw → launch transaction. Zero external
   capital inflow to the launch path.
2. **PAIRING IS CONSTRUCTIVE.** Fork test: a V3 `deployTemplate` by a fresh address
   produces a launch whose council-token pair exists, matches the CREATE3-predicted
   address, and is wired as the instance's immutable. Assert predicted == deployed.
3. **ATOMIC ON PAIR FAILURE.** With pair creation forced to revert, `deployTemplate`
   reverts and the caller's USDC is intact — never charged for a failed launch.
4. **OWNER KEEPS THE 2%.** Fork test: post-launch swaps route the full hook fee to
   the launch owner's configured recipient; zero protocol skim beyond the one-time
   deployment fee.
5. **SEAT GATE HOLDS.** No code path executes a seat-directed transfer without the
   ratification mechanism; asserted in the custody-policy suite when it exists,
   and until then by the absence of any such path in deployed bytecode.
6. **ALLOCATION IMMUTABILITY UNTOUCHED.** Existing SAV allocation state is
   byte-identical before and after every deploy in the suite.
